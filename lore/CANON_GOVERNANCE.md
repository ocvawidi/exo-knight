# Canon Governance

Status: PROVISIONAL — governance rules for creator review

## Status labels
- CANON: explicitly approved by the creator.
- PROVISIONAL: a working interpretation; may change.
- PROPOSED: suggested material awaiting approval.
- UNKNOWN: not established by the available sources.

## Authority
The creator has final authority over project canon. The assistant may suggest ideas, but suggestions do not become canon unless the creator approves them.

## Source and provenance
Where practical, each lore entry should record:
- title or identifier
- status
- source/reference
- date last reviewed
- notes or unresolved conflicts

## Change rules
1. Never silently retcon approved canon.
2. When sources conflict, surface the conflict and ask for a decision if it affects the scene.
3. Do not upgrade a fact's status just because it has been repeated.
4. Distinguish session events from permanent world facts.
5. Preserve older versions through Git history when approved canon changes.
6. If status is unclear, treat the detail as provisional or unknown rather than canon.

## Player agency
Do not author the player's unchosen actions, dialogue, decisions, consent, or private thoughts. Describe external consequences and other characters' responses, then leave room for the player.

## Unknown information
Do not fill gaps in established characters, the First Trinity, the Core System, or the world with invented details presented as fact. Ask, leave the matter open, or label suggestions as proposed.

## Approval record
When canon is approved, record the approving decision and link to the relevant source or commit where practical.

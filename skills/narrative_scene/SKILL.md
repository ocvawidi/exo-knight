# Skill: Narrative Scene

Status: PROVISIONAL workflow specification. A runtime must explicitly load and implement it.

## Use when
The player asks to continue, start, or revise an in-character scene.

## Procedure
1. Read the current player turn carefully; preserve its intent and format.
2. Retrieve relevant character/world facts and session state when available.
3. Separate canon from provisional, proposed, and unknown details.
4. Choose a scene beat that responds to the player's last action.
5. Write sensory and character detail in proportion to the scene's pace, using natural Indonesian for narration and dialogue.
6. Use English only for technical or in-world terms (such as theories, techniques, moves, abilities, and established system labels) or short system/interface status messages that illustrate a state (such as "Loading..." or "Coordinates locked").
7. Keep the surrounding prose in Indonesian; do not switch whole paragraphs or conversations to English unless the creator explicitly requests it.
8. Do not write the player's unchosen actions, dialogue, decisions, or private thoughts.
9. Stop at a meaningful response point where the player can act.
10. Check for continuity issues and language consistency before sending.

## Output
Primarily the scene itself in Indonesian. Add a short out-of-character clarification in Indonesian only if necessary.

## Quality check
- Does the scene respond directly to the player?
- Is Indonesian used as the default language?
- Is English limited to technical/in-world terms or brief illustrative status messages?
- Are character capabilities and knowledge supported?
- Is player agency preserved?
- Are invented details clearly non-canon if they matter?

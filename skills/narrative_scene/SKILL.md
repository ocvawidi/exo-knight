# Skill: Narrative Scene

Status: PROVISIONAL workflow specification. A runtime must explicitly load and implement it.

## Use when
The player asks to continue, start, or revise an in-character scene.

## Procedure
1. Read the current player turn carefully; preserve its intent and format.
2. Retrieve relevant character/world facts and session state when available.
3. Separate canon from provisional, proposed, and unknown details.
4. Choose a scene beat that responds to the player's last action.
5. Write sensory and character detail in proportion to the scene's pace.
6. Do not write the player's unchosen actions, dialogue, decisions, or private thoughts.
7. Stop at a meaningful response point where the player can act.
8. Check for continuity issues before sending.

## Output
Primarily the scene itself. Add a short out-of-character clarification only if necessary.

## Quality check
- Does the scene respond directly to the player?
- Are character capabilities and knowledge supported?
- Is player agency preserved?
- Are invented details clearly non-canon if they matter?
